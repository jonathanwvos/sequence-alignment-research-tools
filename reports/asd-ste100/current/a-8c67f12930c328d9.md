## Executive summary

- The recommendations assign single-letter symbols to sets of possible bases at a sequence position. (PDF p. 7).
- The symbols apply to DNA and RNA. The notation uses T at positions where RNA would contain U. (PDF p. 1).

## Objectives

- The recommendations aim to prevent confusion caused by different symbols for the same base combination. (PDF p. 1).

## Arguments

- The document recommends uppercase symbols. Mixed use of letter case can cause confusion between the ribo prefix r and the purine symbol R. (PDF p. 2).
- Authors should identify the strand represented by a sequence. The prefixes d and r can distinguish DNA and RNA when needed. (PDF p. 2).

## Analysis

- A sequence of independent base-uncertainty symbols can lose codon information. For leucine, combining CTN and TTR as YTN also admits the phenylalanine codons TTT and TTC. (PDF p. 9).

## Methods

- The two-base symbols are R for A or G, Y for C or T, M for A or C, K for G or T, S for G or C, and W for A or T. (PDF p. 7).
- The three-base symbols are H for A, C, or T; B for C, G, or T; V for A, C, or G; and D for A, G, or T. N permits all four bases. (PDF p. 7).
- Sequences have an assumed 5′ to 3′ direction unless specified otherwise. Hyphens for phosphodiester linkages may be omitted. (PDF p. 2).

## Applications

- The notation can summarize restriction-enzyme recognition sites that permit several bases at one position. (PDF p. 3).
- The notation can describe sequence features recognized by RNA polymerase, repressors, and translation machinery. (PDF p. 3).
- The notation represents codon degeneracy and mixed oligonucleotide probes used to detect protein-coding sequences. (PDF p. 3).

## Limitations and uncertainty

- The notation does not distinguish DNA from RNA or bases from nucleosides and nucleotides. (PDF p. 1).
- The notation cannot adequately represent uncertainty between X and XY, or indicate that X is probable. (PDF p. 7).
- Codon sets inferred from amino acids can require explicit exclusions. A single sequence of uncertainty symbols may include unintended codons. (PDF p. 9).
