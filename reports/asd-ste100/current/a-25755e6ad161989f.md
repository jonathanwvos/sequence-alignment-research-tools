## Executive summary

- The authors prove lower bounds for LCS sketching and streaming, and settle the fine-grained complexity of weighted LCS. (PDF p. 1).
- Under SETH, computing weighted LCS requires (nm)^(1-o(1)) time over constant-size alphabets. (PDF p. 4).

## Objectives

- Establish fundamental complexity bounds for subsequence sketching, streaming, and weighted LCS computation. (PDF p. 1).

## Arguments

- Subsequence sketching bounds directly imply lower bounds for streaming and approximate LCS computation. (PDF p. 3).

## Analysis

- Fine-grained reductions from the Orthogonal Vectors Hypothesis bound WLCS running time under SETH. (PDF p. 14).

## Methods

- Communication complexity reductions establish Omega(L^(|Sigma|-1) log L) lower bounds for deterministic subsequence sketches. (PDF p. 3).
- String gadgets over fixed alphabets encode vector orthogonality into optimal weighted alignment scores. (PDF p. 13).

## Applications

- Theoretical foundations of string comparison algorithms and fine-grained complexity analysis. (PDF p. 2).

## Results

- Proves matching upper and lower bounds for subsequence sketching and establishes conditional optimality of O(nm) WLCS. (PDF p. 4).

## Limitations and uncertainty

- Results show that sublinear sketching or strongly subquadratic algorithms are impossible for general alphabets under SETH. (PDF p. 2).
