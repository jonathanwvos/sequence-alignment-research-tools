## Executive summary

- The authors introduce CLUSTAL_X, a graphical user interface for the ClustalW multiple sequence alignment engine. (PDF p. 1).
- CLUSTAL_X provides interactive visualization, residue quality scoring, and partial realignment of divergent segments. (PDF p. 2).

## Objectives

- Facilitate interactive quality assessment and local realignment of multiple sequence alignments in a windowed environment. (PDF p. 2).

## Arguments

- Visualizing quality profiles allows users to identify misaligned regions and refine them without realigning the entire dataset. (PDF p. 6).

## Analysis

- Column quality is evaluated by comparing individual sequence residues to profile matrices of aligned sequences. (PDF p. 5).

## Methods

- Quality curves score alignment columns against profile matrices, highlighting low-scoring segments and exceptional residues. (PDF p. 5).
- Users can lock reliable blocks and iteratively realign selected subsets of sequences or columns. (PDF p. 6).

## Applications

- Biological sequence curation and evolutionary analysis of complex multi-domain protein families. (PDF p. 2).

## Optimization

Not stated in the extractable source pages.

## Evaluation and testing

- The tool is demonstrated on alignments of elongation factor EFTU proteins with annotated exceptional residues. (PDF p. 3).

## Results

- Interactive profile realignment significantly improves alignment accuracy across difficult or misaligned regions. (PDF p. 7).

## Limitations and uncertainty

- Manual inspection and realignment require expert user judgment and remain subjective. (PDF p. 7).
