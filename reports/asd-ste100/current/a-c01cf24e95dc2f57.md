## Executive summary

- The authors develop Foldseek-Multimer to align multimeric protein complexes at high speed. (PDF p. 1).
- The algorithm combines Foldseek pairwise chain comparisons with an efficient chain pairing heuristic. (PDF p. 1).

## Objectives

- Enable fast structural alignment and search across massive multimeric protein complex databases. (PDF p. 1).

## Arguments

- Structural alphabets and prefiltering reduce the combinatorial complexity of complex-to-complex alignment. (PDF p. 1).

## Analysis

- The authors evaluate runtime and interface alignment quality across thousands of protein assemblies. (PDF p. 2).

## Methods

- The method computes pairwise chain alignments using Foldseek 3Di and amino acid alphabets. (PDF p. 5).
- Chains are paired and ordered using a greedy cluster-based concatenation heuristic. (PDF p. 6).

## Applications

- Large-scale clustering and searching of complex structures in the AlphaFold and 3DComplex databases. (PDF p. 2).

## Optimization

- Prefiltering chains by structural similarity reduces candidate pairings before full superposition. (PDF p. 2).

## Evaluation and testing

- Foldseek-Multimer is benchmarked against US-align on the 3DComplexV7 database. (PDF p. 2).

## Results

- Foldseek-Multimer runs three to four orders of magnitude faster than US-align with comparable accuracy. (PDF p. 2).

## Limitations and uncertainty

- Complexes with extensive chain interleaving or conformational changes can show reduced interface scores. (PDF p. 2).
