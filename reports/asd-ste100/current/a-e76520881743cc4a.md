## Executive summary

- The authors extend sparse dynamic programming to non-linear, convex, and concave gap cost functions. (PDF p. 1).
- The framework solves RNA structure prediction and sequence alignment over M sparse points in subquadratic time. (PDF p. 3).

## Objectives

- Generalize sparse dynamic programming to non-linear cost functions without incurring full quadratic overhead. (PDF p. 2).

## Arguments

- Convex and concave cost functions preserve geometric dominance properties that permit sparse evaluation. (PDF p. 3).

## Analysis

- Total monotonicity of the cost matrices allows efficient column minima searches over candidate match points. (PDF p. 18).

## Methods

- Matrix searching techniques exploit Monge and total monotonicity properties of convex and concave functions. (PDF p. 18).
- Diagonal sweeps partition the search domain into independent forward and backward influence regions. (PDF p. 18).

## Applications

- RNA secondary structure prediction with convex or concave loop energy functions and fragment alignment. (PDF p. 21).

## Optimization

- Partitioning ranges across diagonal sweeps removes absolute value complications in distance metrics. (PDF p. 18).

## Evaluation and testing

Not stated in the extractable source pages.

## Results

- Yields an O(n + M log M log log min(M, n^2/M)) algorithm for RNA single loops under convex or concave energy functions. (PDF p. 3).

## Limitations and uncertainty

- Time bounds include additional logarithmic factors compared to strictly linear cost functions. (PDF p. 3).
