## Executive summary

- The authors introduce T-Coffee for progressive multiple sequence alignment with reduced greedy error accumulation. (PDF p. 1).
- T-Coffee combines heterogeneous pairwise alignments into a consistency library to guide progressive alignment. (PDF p. 1).

## Objectives

- Overcome greedy heuristic errors in progressive alignment by incorporating global consistency information. (PDF p. 2).

## Arguments

- Combining local and global alignment information allows identifying consistent residues before tree progression. (PDF p. 12).

## Analysis

- Accuracy is scored by measuring the percentage of trusted benchmark columns correctly reproduced. (PDF p. 7).

## Methods

- A primary library of pairwise alignments is generated using ClustalW and Lalign. (PDF p. 3).
- Library extension computes triplet consistency scores between all sequence pairs before guide tree progression. (PDF p. 6).

## Applications

- Multiple alignment of divergent protein and nucleotide sequences. (PDF p. 1).

## Optimization

Not stated in the extractable source pages.

## Evaluation and testing

- T-Coffee is benchmarked against ClustalW, Prrp, and Dialign on the BAliBASE database across five test categories. (PDF p. 10).

## Results

- T-Coffee significantly outperforms existing progressive aligners, especially on sequences with less than 30 percent identity. (PDF p. 10).

## Limitations and uncertainty

- Triplet library extension requires O(N^3 L^2) time and memory, limiting scalability on very large sequence sets. (PDF p. 11).
