Local collection

Each row opens the local PDF or its place in the citation graph. The citation counts include only title matches in other PDFs’ reference sections. Downstream reach counts all connected later papers, including indirect paths.

Trace the lineage

The corpus overview shows every indexed paper, including undated and unlinked papers. Changing the root, direction or depth switches to the dependency tree, which shows papers within the chosen number of steps. Arrows run from a cited paper to the paper that cites it.

Corpus overview

A paper is “unlinked” when no other indexed local paper matches its title in an extracted reference section and it has no matched local references of its own. This does not establish that it has no citations elsewhere. Degree counts direct incoming and outgoing citation links in the full local collection. The hub toggle marks the top 10% by degree, including ties. This is a citation graph of papers held locally, not proof that an algorithm technically depends on every cited work. Exact title matching misses abbreviated, OCR damaged, or absent references. Undated papers remain visible in the list; chronology is provisional where the year comes from PDF front matter.
